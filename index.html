    Every answer comes from a precomputed database of all 32,246 moon sign changes and 72,553 nakshatra changes between 1900 and 2100, calculated with Swiss Ephemeris (Lahiri ayanamsa) to the second. Most dates have one moon sign. On about 2 days in 5 the Moon changes sign during the day; the card then shows the exact changeover time, and a birth time settles it.

    Sidereal positions with the Lahiri ayanamsa, mean Rahu and Ketu, whole-sign houses. Planets are calculated in the app with Astronomy Engine and match Swiss Ephemeris to within about one arcminute; the lagna to within a few arcseconds. The time zone comes from the birthplace’s historical records, including India’s wartime clock of 1942–45. If the family used a different local time, change the clock setting under the place.
